Workflow Optimization

  1. Outline first: Generate structure, then flesh out sections
  2. Iterate: Refine with follow-up prompts
  3. Human review: Always edit AI-generated content
  4. Batch processing: Generate multiple pieces at once

Cost-Saving Tips

  • Use GPT-4o mini for simple content (4x cheaper)
  • Generate outlines with cheap models, drafts with premium
  • Reuse and refine rather than regenerating
  • Use few-shot examples to improve quality
