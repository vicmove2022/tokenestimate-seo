AI Token Usage by Industry

See how finance, healthcare, legal, and other industries use AI tokens in practice.

Aug 16, 2026 · 11 min read

Industry-Specific AI Usage

Different industries use AI differently, leading to varying token consumption patterns. Here's what we've observed across sectors.

Finance & Banking

Financial institutions use AI for:

  • Market analysis: Processing news, reports, and financial data
  • Risk assessment: Analyzing credit risk and fraud detection
  • Customer service: Chatbots for banking queries
  • Compliance: Regulatory document review

Average tokens per query: 2,000-10,000 (due to long financial documents)

Monthly cost (10K users): $2,000-10,000

Healthcare

Healthcare applications include:

  • Medical literature review: Processing research papers
  • Patient communication: Explaining diagnoses and treatments
  • Administrative tasks: Insurance coding, documentation
  • Drug discovery: Analyzing chemical compounds

Average tokens per query: 5,000-20,000 (medical documents are long)

Monthly cost (10K users): $5,000-20,000

Legal

Legal AI use cases:

  • Contract review: Analyzing lengthy agreements
  • Case research: Searching precedents and statutes
  • Document drafting: Creating legal documents
  • Due diligence: Reviewing thousands of pages

Average tokens per query: 10,000-50,000 (legal documents are very long)

Monthly cost (10K users): $10,000-50,000

E-commerce & Retail

  • Product descriptions: Generating listings
  • Customer support: Answering product questions
  • Personalization: Recommendation engines
  • Review analysis: Summarizing customer feedback

Average tokens per query: 500-2,000

Monthly cost (10K users): $500-2,000

Education

  • Tutoring: Personalized learning assistance
  • Content creation: Lesson plans and materials
  • Grading: Essay and assignment review
  • Research: Literature review assistance

Average tokens per query: 1,000-5,000

Monthly cost (10K users): $1,000-5,000
