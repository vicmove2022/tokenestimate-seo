AI Hallucination Guide

Understand AI hallucinations, learn to detect them, and implement prevention strategies.

Aug 16, 2026 · 10 min read

What are AI Hallucinations?

AI hallucinations occur when language models generate false, misleading, or nonsensical information that sounds plausible. This is one of the biggest challenges in production AI systems.

Types of Hallucinations

TypeExampleFrequency
Factual errors"The Eiffel Tower was built in 1889" (correct)Common
Fabricated citationsMaking up paper titles and authorsV/common
Logical errorsIncorrect math or reasoningCommon
ContradictionsSaying conflicting thingsOccasional
Detailed nonsenseComplex but wrong explanationsOccasional

Causes of Hallucinations

  1. Training data gaps: Missing information leads to guesses
  2. Ambiguous prompts: Vague instructions cause misunderstandings
  3. Model limitations: Models optimize for coherence, not truth
  4. Context length: Longer contexts increase error risk

How to Reduce Hallucinations

  1. Grounded generation: Use RAG with verified sources
  2. Citation requirements: Ask models to cite sources
  3. Few-shot examples: Provide accurate examples
  4. Verification step: Add human or automated fact-checking
  5. Confidence scoring: Flag uncertain responses

When to Be Careful

  • Medical advice - always verify with professionals
  • Legal information - confirm with legal experts
  • Factual research - cross-check important claims
  • Financial advice - validate with professionals
