The Short Answer

Why the Ratio Varies

Tokens are not words. A tokenizer breaks text into subword units based on frequency. Common words like "the" become single tokens, while rare words split into multiple tokens.

Factors affecting tokenization:

  • Language — Chinese characters = 1-2 tokens each
  • Vocabulary size — Larger vocabularies = shorter tokens
  • Special characters — Code, math symbols tokenize differently
  • Whitespace — Spaces attach to following tokens

Quick Estimation Formulas

Use these rules of thumb when you need fast estimates:

  • English: tokens ≈ words × 1.3
  • Code: tokens ≈ words × 1.1 (more efficient)
  • Chinese: tokens ≈ characters × 1.5
  • Japanese: tokens ≈ characters × 1.2

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a fixed token-to-word ratio?

No. The ratio varies by language, vocabulary, and text type. English averages 1.3 tokens per word, but code and technical text can differ.

How does Chinese text tokenize?

Chinese characters typically tokenize as 1-2 tokens each. Punctuation and whitespace follow similar rules to English.
