What are Content Clusters?

Content clusters organize your website around topics. A pillar page covers a broad topic, supported by cluster pages covering subtopics.

Benefits of Content Clusters

  1. Builds topical authority
  2. Improves internal linking
  3. Enhances user experience
  4. Helps search engines understand your site

Creating Pillar Content

  • Comprehensive coverage (3,000+ words)
  • Clear navigation to cluster pages
  • Internal links from all cluster pages
  • Regular updates to maintain relevance
