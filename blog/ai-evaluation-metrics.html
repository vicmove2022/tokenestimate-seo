AI Evaluation Metrics

Measure and optimize AI model performance with the right evaluation metrics.

Aug 16, 2026 · 10 min read

Why Evaluate AI Models?

Evaluating AI models ensures they meet quality standards before deployment. Without proper evaluation, you risk poor user experience and wasted costs.

Key Evaluation Metrics

MetricWhat It MeasuresTarget
AccuracyCorrectness of responses>90%
RelevanceHow well response matches query>85%
ToxicityHarmful/inappropriate content<5%
HallucinationFactual errors<10%
Response timeTime to first token<2s
Cost per queryAverage API costMinimize

Evaluation Methods

  1. Automated tests: Compare against golden answers
  2. LLM-as-judge: Use another model to evaluate responses
  3. Human evaluation: Expert review of samples
  4. A/B testing: Compare model versions in production

Cost-Quality Tradeoff Analysis

Balance quality improvements against cost increases:

Value Score = Quality / Cost

GPT-4o mini: Quality 7/10, Cost $0.15/1M → Score 46.7
GPT-4o:      Quality 9/10, Cost $2.50/1M → Score 3.6
Claude Haiku: Quality 7/10, Cost $0.80/1M → Score 8.75

  • Monitor cost per query trends
  • Alert on quality degradation
  • Compare model performance monthly
