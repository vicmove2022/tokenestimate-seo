Keyword Research for AI Tools

Find high-value keywords to drive traffic to your AI tool website.

Aug 16, 2026 · 9 min read

Keyword Research Process

  1. Brainstorm seed keywords: Core topics related to your tool
  2. Use keyword tools: Ahrefs, SEMrush, Google Keyword Planner
  3. Analyze search intent: Informational vs transactional
  4. Check competition: Domain authority and content quality
  5. Create content plan: Map keywords to pages
