Internal Linking Strategy

Boost your SEO rankings with strategic internal linking.

Aug 16, 2026 · 10 min read

Why Internal Linking Matters

  • Helps search engines discover and index content
  • Distributes page authority (link equity) across site
  • Improves user navigation and engagement
  • Establishes content hierarchy

Linking Best Practices

PracticeDoDon't
Anchor textDescriptive keywords"Click here"
Link depthWithin 3 clicksDeep nested pages
RelevanceContextually relatedRandom links
Number10-15 per pageLink spam

Link Equity Distribution

Important pages should have more internal links pointing to them:

  • Homepage: Link from all category pages
  • Calculator pages: Link from relevant blog posts
  • Blog posts: Link to related articles
