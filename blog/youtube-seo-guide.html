YouTube SEO Guide 2026

Complete guide to optimizing YouTube videos for search and recommendations.

Aug 16, 2026 · 11 min read

YouTube SEO Factors (2026)

FactorWeightOptimization Tip
Watch time40%Create engaging content that keeps viewers
CTR25%Optimize thumbnails and titles
Session time15%End screens and playlists
Keywords10%Include in title, description, tags
Engagement10%Encourage likes, comments, shares

Title Optimization

  • Include primary keyword near the beginning
  • Keep under 60 characters
  • Create curiosity gaps
  • Use numbers and power words

Description Best Practices

  1. First 2 lines contain main keywords
  2. Add timestamps for longer videos
  3. Include relevant links
  4. Use 3-5 relevant hashtags

Thumbnail Optimization

  • High contrast, clear text overlay
  • Facial expressions increase CTR
  • Consistent branding across videos
  • A/B test different thumbnails
