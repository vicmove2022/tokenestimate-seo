Strengths by Provider

OpenAI

  • Best ecosystem and documentation
  • Strong reasoning (o1, o3 models)
  • Excellent coding assistance
  • Wide model selection

Anthropic

  • Largest context window (200K)
  • Excellent at long document analysis
  • Claude's constitutional AI approach
  • Strong safety focus

Google

  • Longest context window (1M tokens)
  • Most cost-effective for large datasets
  • Integrated with Google Cloud
  • Strong multimodal capabilities
